Consent & Privacy Agreement

Please read and agree before we collect your health information

Patient Consent for Health Data Collection

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the NDHM guidelines, you have full control over your health data.


What we collect: Your symptoms, medical history, uploaded documents, and basic demographic information linked to your ABHA ID.


How it is used: Processed by AI to generate a structured medical history summary. Shared ONLY with the attending doctor at this facility during your visit today.


Data security: All data encrypted (AES-256). Session data permanently deleted after your doctor confirms your summary.


Your rights: You can revoke consent at any time. The AI summary is a DRAFT only. Your doctor verifies before use. It is NOT an autonomous diagnosis.


Grievance: Contact facility health officer or call 1800-11-4477 (Toll Free).
